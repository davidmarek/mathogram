# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Elementary-school children practicing arithmetic at home or school.

## Product Purpose

Mathixel turns arithmetic practice into discovering original pixel pictures.
Children choose a picture, solve equations, and reveal one colored square per
correct answer. Success means enjoying practice and completing a picture.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the gallery, game, completion screens, Czech and English translations,
difficulty and exercise settings, row hints, keyboard and touch keypad input,
light/dark/system appearance, offline support, and saved progress.
Existing attempts retain their exact equations and picture coordinates.
No accounts, ads, penalties, visible timers, sound, or external artwork.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Mathixel name and original pixel artwork. The experience should feel
like a fun game for children, not a generic AI-generated website.

## Evidence on Hand

Original artwork in `src/content/animals.ts`, localized copy in `src/i18n`,
and implemented game rules documented in README.md.

## Product Principles

- Put choosing and revealing pictures ahead of explanatory chrome.
- Preserve progress and make mistakes safe.
- Keep arithmetic and game state clear for young readers.
- Give touch, keyboard, and assistive-technology users the same game.
